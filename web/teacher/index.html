Slicing is closed.

Students can open and set up models any time. Slicing only works while you have it open, with this phrase on the board. The slicer (and its bill) sleeps the rest of the time: one minute after the last slice, or right away when you press Close now. When a class slices at once, students wait in line and each one sees their place.
